# THE WORLD TODAY — Wednesday 25 November 2026

A one-page atlas dashboard summing up Wednesday 25 November 2026 world news: 38 dated stories in 8 regions, for make.fun's news-image-20261125 bounty ($100 in USDC, 50 places paid).

Three lead cards — two oil tankers hit mines in the Strait of Hormuz, the Dow erasing a 1,100-point intraday drop to close at 50,182.95, and US tariffs proposed on 14 trading partners — sit above a market heatmap strip and eight colour-coded region panels (Middle East, Europe, Asia, Americas, Africa, Markets, Science, Sport), with a news ticker and a sources footer. Every story is dated 25 November 2026; earlier events appear only as that day's fallout.

The image is drawn from scratch by `gen_image.py` (Python Pillow, DejaVu fonts, no downloaded images) and copied to the output directory by `main.ts`. Running the program twice writes byte-identical output.
